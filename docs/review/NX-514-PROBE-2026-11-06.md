# NX-514 probe: why the overnight Static never flips a node (2026-11-06)

Tracker NX-514, owner decision Q-TD-143, target Q-TD-71. A causal probe, not a fix: no kernel file, config key or content file changed. Numbers marked **(probe)** are printed by `tools/balance/probe_strikes.py` (`summarize`) from the saved run JSON and can be regenerated with the commands in §2. Numbers marked **(ad hoc)** came from throwaway scripts that are not committed and cannot be regenerated from the repo. What was not run is marked **[UNVERIFIED]**.

## 1. The question

Q0b (#648, BALANCE-LOG) found 0 flips in 720 overnight-0 seed-hours: 119 Offensives, 37 strikes fired, 0 captures. Q-TD-143 (owner, 2026-11-05) wants about 1 flip per 12 h at 0 viewers (Q-TD-71: slow but real progress, at most 1 per 12 h, PROPOSED, within A3). Why do 82 of the 119 planned night strikes never fire, and which one config key should a same-sha A/B test?

## 4. Causal chain

1. **One Offensive per 6 h, always at Pattern of Life.** `launch.py:444-449` runs the capture slot at `director.pol.capture_slot_tiers` (`balance/default.yaml:382`, PATTERN_OF_LIFE) once `director.pol.offensive_period_s` (`default.yaml:374`, 21600) has passed. With 0 viewers the tier never rises, so this is the only attack the Static makes: 12 per seed per 72 h.
2. **It always picks the forward airbase.** Candidates at the start: `west-airbase`, `fob-n-w`, `fob-s-w` (**ad hoc**, fresh campaign, seed 1, one `ops.rank` call: scores 1.467, 1.098, 1.0937; not regenerable from the repo). `operations.py:527` scores a candidate as priority x `_node_factors` x memory x noise; `operations.py:493` uses `director.score.target_value.<kind>` (`default.yaml:310-314`: AIRBASE 1.5, FOB 1.0). `noise_scale` is 0.1 (`default.yaml:341`), so 1.5 beats 1.0 on every draw. 119 of 119 launches targeted west-airbase (probe).
3. **That node cannot be taken at 0 viewers, by rule.** `capture.py:134-145` (`_floor`): with no live viewer unit the theater's forward airbase (`content/theaters/strait-1.yaml:76`, west-airbase) is skipped: its bar never starts, fills or decays (Q-GP-28, Q-TD-93). So even a perfect strike and capture at west-airbase flips nothing. Aiming every Offensive at it makes the drift 0 by construction.
4. **The strike dies on the way in.** west-airbase holds the SAM site `ad-west` (`strait-1.yaml:39`; SAM `content/weapons.yaml:11`: 35 km range, `base_pk` 0.7, 8 rounds). Seed 1 trace (**ad hoc**; a launch that fired, so it shows the shot-down jets, not the no-shot group): both strike fighters fly in at x 159850, the SAM fires from tick 219533, hits unit 59 at 219629 (MISSION_KILL, released at 219650) and unit 60 at 219704 (released at 219750); unit 60 got off one AG_LIGHT shot at 219699 (a miss) and was destroyed at 219784. In the 73 launches of the first table row no jet fired before it was lost; in the 37 that fired, the jets were lost after 1 to 7 shots. Two jets with 4 AG_LIGHT rounds each (`ammo [4, 400]`) are all the Offensive buys (`content/operations.yaml`, OFFENSIVE: STRIKE slots 2).
5. **Fired strikes rarely bring defenses to 0, because the claimants die.** `tasking.py:753-` ends a STRIKE job DONE only at 0 defense HP; a claimed job does not expire, an unclaimed one does after `tasking.expire_min` (15 min, `default.yaml:183`; `tasking.py:822`). In 36 of the 37 fired launches the job expired with the defenses up after every claimant had been SAM-hit (probe), and the CAPTURE job waiting on it then FAILED (`tasking.py:889-893` frees dependents only on DONE). The 1 launch that reached DONE had all its claimants SAM-hit as well and still took the defenses to 0.

## 6. Recommended A/B

- **Key:** `director.score.target_value.AIRBASE`
- **Current default:** 1.5 (`balance/default.yaml:311`)
- **Proposed:** 0.5. Any value from 0.5 to 0.8 should act the same at full defenses (an airbase scores at most 0.8 x 1.1 = 0.88, a FOB at least 1.0 x 0.9 = 0.9); the check arm measured only 0.5. Make 0.5 arm B; a second arm at 0.75 is optional.
- **Why:** steps 2 and 3 of the chain. At the shipped value the only attack the sleeping Static makes goes at a node that cannot be taken, through a SAM it cannot beat. The key moves that attack to a node that can fall and has no SAM. It is the one number that changes the target without touching the idle floor (a decided rule, Q-GP-28), the night cap, or the unit catalog.
- **Not enough alone, probably:** at 0.5 the drift reached one coalition flip per 36 h in 144 seed-hours. If the 10-seed A/B stays under the target, the next candidates the data points at are `campaign.night_progress_cap_nodes_per_h` (`default.yaml:176`, step 5 above, the second blocker) and the Offensive's strike strength (`content/operations.yaml`: 2 jets, a content change by the `new-operation` skill, not a balance key). Neither was run. **[UNVERIFIED]**.

## 7. Risks (what else the key moves)

- `target_value` ranks every node-targeted Static operation at every tier: OFFENSIVE, RETAKE and PROBE (applied in `_node_factors`, `operations.py:474-488`; `_foe_value`, `:491-499`, holds the lookup), and, through `fed_nodes`, the STRIKE_BRIDGE value (`operations.py:480-482`). At ALPHA and BRAVO the Static will pressure FOBs ahead of the forward airbase.
- With one viewer or more the forward airbase is no longer skipped (`capture.py:143`), so quiet-5, prime-50 and the higher crowds shift Static pressure off west-airbase. That could soften the A1 no-collapse rule or change win times at evening-150, raid-300 and surge-500 (the A1 and A3 checks, Q-TEST-13). **[UNVERIFIED]**: not run. The harness block therefore asks for overnight-0, quiet-5 and prime-50.
- A3 (at most one strategic node lost in the first 12 h at 0 viewers): no coalition flip fell inside 12 h in the 4 check seeds, but 4 seeds say little. Read it on the 10 seeds.
- The "last base" rule (`capture.py:148-157`) still protects the coalition's final strategic node, so the A1 floor holds at 0 viewers by design.
- Crossing flips (section 5) may or may not count as drift; see the question there.
