### Findings
1. **The plateau is the two east FOBs, every time.** All 8 stalled seed-arms end hour 4 holding the other six strategic nodes and missing exactly fob-n-e and fob-s-e. N0's pooled hint was right.
2. **The victory threshold needs at least one of them.** `campaign.victory_node_frac` 0.80 of 8 strategic nodes means 7 (`balance/default.yaml:151`). 9 of the 16 won seed-arms win holding 7 with one east FOB still Static. A seed wins when it takes one east FOB and keeps it for the 30-minute hold, and stalls when it can't.
3. **Travel time alone doesn't explain it.** The east FOBs are 18.8 game minutes from the Static's rear source, within the hold, but east-airbase (11.8 min) and the crossings (25.7 min) are within it too, and the coalition owns those 2.4 to 6.7 times as long (pooled minutes above). What sets the east FOBs apart in the theater file: each is one road link from the Static HQ (`content/theaters/strait-1.yaml:57-58`; the HQ has garrison quota 8, `:35`), starts Static-owned with garrison quota 4 and a short-range air-defense site (`strait-1.yaml:32-33, :44-45`).
4. **Most east FOB losses follow no known launch.** In arm A, 18 of 25 east FOB losses came with no Static capture operation known on the node; in arm B, 35 of 48. Which units take them (the HQ garrison, leftover armor, convoys) is [UNVERIFIED]: the "Who takes coalition nodes" table isn't split by node.
5. **Agency doesn't change which nodes.** Arm B stalls on the same pair; it only tips more seeds into the stall (L0, N0).

## What this means

On this map, a raid wins if it takes and holds one of the two FOBs next to the Static HQ, and stalls if it can't. The stall is a map and threshold question, not a Director or hauling one. The owner already said on Q-TD-136 that raids ending a little sooner is fine ("balance as we go on this small map"). So the useful choices are to lower the victory threshold to 6 of 8, to make the east FOBs easier to take and hold, or to keep the stall.

## Decisions (lead; owner may veto)
- **No rule change.** Q-TD-142 asks the owner which way to go, with a recommendation.
- **Measure the threshold option first (harness only):** a 12-seed run of raid-300 and raid-300-agency with `set: campaign.victory_node_frac=0.75` (6 of 8), at the same sha. It shows whether the stalled seeds then win, and whether the won seeds become steamrolls (Q-TEST-11 flags). This needs no code; the queue's `set` key passes the override.
- **An east FOB change (garrison or air defense) is content,** so it can only be measured after an owner-approved content PR merges.
