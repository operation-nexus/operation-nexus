The lead wrote this review from report comment 6623043625 on #622.

### Findings
1. **The stalls end.** All 8 O0 stalls win, 7 of them within 4.53 h. Arm B, which stalled half its seeds, wins all 12.
2. **No steamrolls, and wins come a little sooner.** No won seed is flagged in either arm. Of the 15 seed-arms that won in both runs, 14 win sooner or the same (A s6 2.13 → 2.12 h) and one later (A s4 1.76 → 2.91 h). The shortest win is 1.75 h, against 1.76 h in O0.
3. **The coalition now wins on the six it already held.** Of the 21 won seed-arms that ended within the first 4 hours, 12 won holding exactly the six nodes that aren't east FOBs (A s3, s4, s8, s9, s12; B s1, s3, s4, s5, s9, s10, s12). The east FOBs stay the least-held nodes (about 350–370 coalition-owned minutes per arm).
4. **The see-saw can still happen, now one step lower.** A s10, which won at 2.58 h in O0, doesn't end: 53 strategic-node losses and 13 holds started. It spends 151.5 minutes at 6 or more, with a longest stretch of 30.9 minutes, and 40.8 paused minutes from DEFENSES_DOWN. B s8 wins only at 22.23 h after 42 losses and 11 holds.
5. **A2 isn't comparable here.** The A2 medians rise (482 and 573 s, against 293 and 324 in N0/O0), but A2 is pooled over newbies, and most of them arrive in seeds that run the full 24 h. With fewer of those, the pool changes; it isn't a sign of worse play.

## What this means

Lowering the threshold to 6 of 8 does what O0 suggested: a raid no longer has to crack an east FOB next to the Static HQ, so 23 of 24 raids end, and none of them as a flagged steamroll. The median win comes 0.4 to 0.8 h sooner, and the fastest stays at about 1¾ h. It doesn't remove the see-saw; a seed can still stall between 5 and 6 (A s10), but that's 1 seed in 24 instead of 8. The owner already said on Q-TD-136 that raids ending a little sooner is fine.
