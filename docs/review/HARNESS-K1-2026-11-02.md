### Findings
1. **The won seeds never go backward.** None of them ever drops below 3 strategic nodes, and every one reaches a stretch of at least 30 minutes at 7 or more, which is what the hold needs. They lose a strategic node 1–5 times.
2. **The stalled seeds see-saw.** s8 and s9 spend about 5–6 hours each down at 1–2 strategic nodes, below the 3 that no won seed ever drops under, and also hours at 6. s8 reaches 7 for 24.5 minutes in total but never for longer than 15.1 at a stretch; s9 never reaches 7 at all. They lose strategic nodes 38 and 35 times: 73 of the run's 97 losses.
3. **The contested nodes are the ones the Static can reach in time.** Pooled over seeds, the crossings (cross-n 22 losses, cross-s 21), the east FOBs (fob-n-e 12, fob-s-e 13) and east-airbase (9) change hands most. Those are the nodes the report's travel table marks as reachable by Static armor within the 30-minute hold. The far-west nodes it marks as unreachable within the hold (fob-n-w 10 losses, fob-s-w 10, west-airbase 0) are held longest; west-airbase was never lost.
4. **About half the losses follow no Static capture operation.** 45 losses came after an OFFENSIVE and 2 after a RETAKE (these match the capture funnel's 45 and 2 captures), but 50 had no capture launch on record. Which Static units take those nodes is [UNVERIFIED]; the report can't name the capturer, because `NodeCaptured` names no Static unit.

## What this means

A raid stalls when the fight turns into a see-saw over the nodes the Static can reach quickly, and the coalition is pushed back far enough to lose ground it normally keeps. It isn't the hold rules (K0) and it isn't money (W7): the coalition can't keep 7 of 8 nodes for 30 minutes. Why s8 and s9 tip into the see-saw and the other seeds don't is [UNVERIFIED]. Their engagement numbers (A2 about 200 s, idle 5–6%) say the players are busy, not absent.
