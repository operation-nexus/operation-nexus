Every raid victory in these runs is a domination hold. So NX-490 makes no measurable difference at these profiles. The T0/T1 1-commander HQ grabs (2 of 2) came from earlier code and seeds. Whether NX-490 prevents them is [UNVERIFIED], because no run here made the attempt.
2. **Raid-300 still ends in about 2.4 h.** All 6 seeds won.
   - Median: 2.4 h (V2 2.5).
   - Seeds 2, 3 and 4 are unchanged at 2.48, 2.23 and 2.51 h.

   - NX-489 decay did not lengthen raids. The hold counts do not move. Whether broken holds resumed or reset can't be read, because the report does not yet print `holds_resumed` or `hold_resets` (the NX-489 follow-up).
3. **Raid A2 got slightly worse.** The median first impact is 801 s against 640 s. The share over 900 s is 48.3% against 48.0%. The cause is [UNVERIFIED], since the pooled seeds differ (H3).
4. **Quiet-5 shows the first `SANCTUARY_AT_RISK` sitrep** (1 in 6 seeds, NX-493). Every other quiet-5, prime-50 and overnight-0 number printed is identical to V2.

   These are within seed noise as earlier reviews have seen it.

## Decisions and follow-ups (lead)
- **Raid length.** This is within the owner's Q-TD-91 ruling: raid wins of any length "as long as they have at least some resistance", and 8 of 14 holds were broken. The real lever for raid length is the domination hold itself (`campaign.victory_hold_*`), not HQ capture or decay. No change for now. A burst-raid profile, landing on a war already in progress (Q-TD-91 (a)), is the better test.
- **NX-490 needs a targeted test** before its values can be judged: a profile or scripted case where a commander actually reaches an HQ. The HQ `time_mult` / `extra_exponent` sweep stays queued behind that.
- **Harness fixes:**
  - `--set` and the queue should accept schema-validated enum strings, after which D2 is re-queued;
  - the report should print NX-489 `holds_resumed` and `hold_resets` by reason (already a follow-up);
  - per-seed A2 and idle armed (H3).
