## Findings
1. **The pause turns raids into fights.** The Static launches nearly four times as many Offensives (97 against 25), loses nearly three times as many strike packages (305 against 112), and breaks twice as many holds (17 against 8). It stays in BRAVO three times longer.
   - 20 of 22 holds paused at least once.
   - The pause reasons are spread evenly across all three conditions.
   - This is the contest Q-TD-84 asked for ("holds are never contested").
2. **Newbie engagement improves sharply.**
   - A2 median 239 s against 801 s; tail 35.2% against 48.3%.
   - Idle armed 17% against 29%.
   - More newbies are counted at ALPHA or above (445 against 147), because raids run longer in BRAVO. The per-seed split (NX-502) isn't in this sha's report.
3. **Won raids barely lengthen** (median 2.5 h against 2.4 h). The spread widens, though: s2 took 5.97 h.
4. **One seed (s1) didn't end in 24 h.** Its holds kept pausing or breaking. This is a possible stalemate. Q-TD-91 allows a raid of any length "as long as they have at least some resistance", but a raid that never closes is a different case. The longest single pause was 64 minutes. Why s1 never closed is [UNVERIFIED]; it needs a per-seed read of holds and pauses.
5. **More starvation** (529 against 296), consistent with a longer, busier war.

## Decisions and follow-ups (lead; owner may veto)
- **Keep the pause on**, as shipped. Its effect is the one the owner agreed to: contested holds, more Offensives and better A2.
- **Q-TD-136 goes to the owner:** should a pause have a limit so a raid can't stall forever? The options are a cap on total paused time per hold, or a cap on any single pause. The lead's recommendation is to wait for more seeds first, because 1 seed in 6 is too little to size a cap. The stalemate check is on the watchlist.
- **Next raid run**, at a sha with NX-502: G0 again with 12 seeds, so the per-seed holds and pauses show what s1 did.
