### Findings
1. **Every capture was seen.** No loss came back `unknown`, and every capturer was a Static Director unit, almost always armor.
2. **About 4 in 10 losses involve Static armor with no job.** 39 losses had at least one capturer holding no job at all. These units aren't part of any operation on the Static's board when they take the node; they capture just by standing in range, which the capture rule allows for any ground unit that isn't a home garrison, a patrol or an AI truck. Where these jobless units come from (for example units left over when their operation ended) is [UNVERIFIED].
3. **K1's "after RETAKE" split undercounted retakes.** K1 attributed only 2 losses to a RETAKE launch, but units on RETAKE jobs were among the capturers at 25 losses. The launch-based split only links a loss to the latest capture launch on that node, so it misses retake units that take a node another way. Exactly why the two disagree is [UNVERIFIED]; the capturer count is the direct measurement and should be read first.
4. **OFFENSIVE units are in 36 losses,** close to the 45 captures the funnel credits to OFFENSIVE launches.

## What this means

The see-saw in the stalled raids (K1) is driven by Static armor. Planned operations (OFFENSIVE and RETAKE) account for most losses, but a large share come from Static armor that holds no job and takes a node simply by being near it. That makes the jobless units the clearest lead so far.

## Addendum: where the jobless armor comes from (code trace, 2026-11-02)

A read-only trace of the kernel, with the key lines checked by the lead:
- When a CAPTURE or DEFEND job ends, `tasking._end` clears the unit's job but leaves its path (`services/sim/src/nexus/core/tasking.py:848-858`); only a point job's DONE sends the unit home and only an INTERCEPT clears the path.
- A CAPTURE job expires at its deadline unless a claimant is actually capturing (`tasking.py:808-814`), so it expires while defenses are up or the bar is stalled, with the armor parked at the target.
- A jobless Director unit is retired after `director.idle_retire_s` 600 s of game time (`balance/default.yaml:319`). An empty base flips in about a minute, so leftover armor has time to take the node once its defenses fall.
- THREAT-DIRECTOR §11 test 23 says a unit with no job does capture, so jobless capture is allowed by the spec; the leftover-after-expiry path is emergent.

Likely sources, ranked by the trace (not yet confirmed per loss): (1) a CAPTURE job expires with its armor parked at the target, which flips the node later; (2) a job ends while the armor is still driving, and it drives on to the target with no job (CAPTURE after its restarts, or DEFEND failed because its node fell); (3) jobless armor drives through a coalition node on its route [UNVERIFIED in K2]. This is recorded as owner question Q-TD-140.
