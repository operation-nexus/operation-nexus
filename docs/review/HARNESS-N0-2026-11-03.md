### Findings
1. **Hour 1 doesn't predict anything.** Every seed starts the same way: Director in BRAVO at minute 20, coalition at 3–6 nodes. A s9, which stalls, has the same hour-1 cell as A s4, s5, s10 and s11, which win (`5/3, 0, 2, 3, BRAVO`).
2. **Every stalled seed sits at exactly 6 of 8 at the end of hour 4.** That holds for all 8 stalled seed-arms (A s8 and s9; B s1, s2, s3, s8, s9 and s10). Every won seed reached 7 or more and won within 4 hours. K1 already showed the stalled seeds spending hours at 6 (A s8: 345 min); the early hours show they reach that plateau by hour 3–4 and stay on it.
3. **The fork is in hours 2–4.** In the four seeds agency tips (s1, s2, s3, s10), arm A reaches 7–8 and wins by hour 4, while arm B ends hour 4 at 6, even where B was ahead at hour 2 (s1 and s2 at 7 against A's 6). At the plateau the coalition mostly stops taking nodes: in hour 4, six of the eight stalled seed-arms capture nothing, and in hour 3 five capture one node or none.
4. **The Static doesn't escalate differently.** First BRAVO is minute 20 in all 24 runs, so the Director's tier doesn't tell the seeds apart. Offensives per hour vary widely in both groups.
5. **Retirements happen at bases.** In arm A, 277 Director units and 1 AI truck retired, and no returning unit was lost. In arm B, 1053 Director units retired and 2 returning units were lost. This fits M0: units rarely need to go home.

## What this means

A raid stalls when the coalition gets stuck at 6 of the 8 strategic nodes by hour 3–4 and never holds a 7th long enough. Agency makes that more likely in hours 2–3: in L0 the tacticians mostly retake lost nodes, and here the arm B seeds that fork fall back from 7 to 6 at that point.
