### Findings
1. **Returning home is rare in raid-300.** Only one seed out of twelve ever had a unit due to retire away from its own base, so the stall picture from K0–K2 stands unchanged.
2. **It doesn't touch the jobless capturers.** Losses with a capturer on no job went from 39 to 40. That fits: a jobless unit is due to retire only after `director.idle_retire_s` (600 s), and an empty base flips in about a minute (K2 addendum), so leftover armor still takes nodes before its retirement time comes.
3. **The report doesn't count returns.** Neither the run JSON nor `report.md` shows how many units went home or how long it took. A count of `RETIRED` despawns, and of returning units lost on the way, would make the next change in this area measurable [harness follow-up, not built].

## Decisions (lead; owner may veto)
- **M0 is the raid-300 baseline from `4b68a06` on.** It matches K2 everywhere except s9.
- **No rule change.** Q-TD-136 stays open with the owner's choice (a).
- **Next (lead, read-only):** trace why the Director's Offensive rate rises with coalition captures (L0 finding 4), as planned.
- **Harness follow-up (small, later):** count `RETIRED` despawns and returning units lost, per profile, in `report.md` (with NX-502's per-seed tables).
