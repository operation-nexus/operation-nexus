# NX-519: where a newbie's time to first impact goes (A2 probe), 2026-11-06

Status: probe result, not a gate. Small samples (4 to 8 seeds per profile); every number is read from `probe_a2.py` output unless marked (derived), (inferred) or [UNVERIFIED]. No kernel, config or content change.

## 1. The question

The Q0c acceptance run (#667, 2901d4c; `docs/review/BALANCE-LOG.md` "Q0c acceptance") fails A2 at every profile with newbies: the tail over 900 s is 16.0 to 50.4 % against a 10 % limit, and the median fails at raid-300 (776 s) and surge-500 (963 s) against 480 s. Where does a newbie's time to first impact go, and which one or two levers would move the tail most?

Already known, not redone here: A2's definition (`tools/balance/README.md` "A2 and impact", TESTING-STRATEGY §3.5a, Q-SIM-93, Q-SIM-97); the per-persona-kind tail and "auto-tasked joiners have no targets" at raid (`ENTERTAINMENT-T0-T1-2026-10-28.md` Q6); scouts and capturers put about 17 % of newbies in the tail on their own, and the owner's fixes NX-512 (sector scout, stale-track refresh) and NX-513 (capture runs from a forward base) (`ENTERTAINMENT-NX-378-LOWCROWD-2026-11-05.md` §5). NX-512 and NX-513 are both in 2901d4c (#662, #655 precede #666), so Q0c already includes them. `refuel_stuck` as a possible part of the raid tail was an open [UNVERIFIED] in T0/T1 item 9.

## 2. Method

**Probe.** `tools/balance/probe_a2.py` (new, with `services/sim/tests/tools/test_probe_a2.py`). It runs `runner.run` with the `on_tick` hook and a tap on `Collector.feed` (the `probe_strikes.py` way), plus a wrapper on `runner._Crowd._lost` to see the moment a harness viewer leaves for good. For every counted newbie (A2's own set: joined with the Static at `a2_min_tier` ALPHA or above) it books each `--sample-s` (5) game seconds to one state, from the first JOIN to the collector's own first-impact tick, so the phases sum to the newbie's time to first impact (a newbie with none: the time watched; test `test_a_run_books_every_second...`).

| Phase | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `join_to_spawn` | no unit yet: queued, JOIN rejected, JOIN just sent | events `ReserveQueueJoined`, `CommandRejected`, `UnitSpawned`; `world.reserve_queue` |
| `after_loss` | lost a unit, waiting to rejoin (persona `rejoin_delay_s`) | events `UnitDestroyed`, `UnitDespawned` |
| `gave_up` | the harness viewer left for good after a loss (`rejoin_prob`, `max_attempts`) | `_Crowd._lost` result (exact) |
| `idle` | a unit with no job and no order: `tasking.idle_diagnosis`'s reason (`full`, `refuel_stuck`, `no_job`, ...); or busy with no job (route, RTB, grounded, HOLD) | world state |
| `transit` | a unit with a job (or a GOTO/FOLLOW order) and still on a path | `Entity.path` (**inferred**: no event says "under way") |
| `on_task` | a unit with a job and no path left (at target or waiting), `fired`/`no_shot` | `tasking._arrived`, `WeaponFired` (**inferred** for "arrived") |

Also recorded per newbie: persona kind, unit kinds, the board job kinds it claimed (`JobClaimed`), each unit lost with its killer (`UnitDestroyed.killer_id`), the first route length and the state timeline. A phase is exact to the 5 s sample. Not in the code, so not measured: spawn refusals by supply or cap. There were none: 0 `CommandRejected` JOINs and 0 queued JOINs in every run below (`JOIN rejections: {}; newbies queued: 0`), so `join_to_spawn` is 0 s everywhere.

## 4. Causal chain

**A. prime-50 (and the other low and mid crowds): the tail is a first sortie that dies, then a viewer who leaves.**

1. A newbie's JOIN spawns a unit at once (`handlers._spawn`, `handlers.py:325` claims the first joinable job) and nothing waits: 0 rejections, 0 queued, 0 s in `join_to_spawn`.
2. 90 of 209 newbies (43 %) lose a unit before their first impact; the first loss comes a median 73 s after the JOIN (24 h). The killers: ENEMY_FIGHTER on STRIKE_FIGHTER 87, ENEMY_STRIKE_FIGHTER on LOGI_TRUCK 21, ENEMY_ARMOR on LOGI_TRUCK 20, ENEMY_FIGHTER on RECON_UAV 16, AIR_DEFENSE_SITE 7 (each unit lost, so a newbie can appear twice). Nearly all in the `transit` state (a STRIKE_FIGHTER's first strike route is a median 212 km, world metres, at 24 h; 277 km at 6 h). At 6 h the killing fighter was mostly not on a job of its own (`no job`) or on a `strike:<node>` job; which Static job it came from was not traced [UNVERIFIED].
3. The harness newbie persona gives up after a loss with probability 0.3, and after 3 consecutive losses: `profiles.yaml:63` (`rejoin_prob: 0.7`, `max_attempts: 3`), `personas.py:88-94` `rejoin_at`, `runner.py:201-205` `_Crowd._lost` (status GONE, no more JOINs). 50 of the 90 who lost a unit gave up (24 h).
4. A2 counts such a newbie as a miss (watched past 900 s with no impact): all 50 are in the tail, 50 of the 70 (71 %); with them left out the 24 h prime tail reads 20/157 = 12.7 % (derived), and at 6 h 6/61 = 9.8 % (derived).
5. The role kinds (capturer, scout, hauler) are 37 of the 70 prime tail newbies (per-kind table; how many of them also gave up was not counted separately), with long transits: first capture-run route median 99.8 km, scout 224.9 km; transit median for capturers 653 s, scouts 897 s (all newbies of the kind, 24 h).

**B. raid-300 (and surge-500, [UNVERIFIED] there): the tail is the Tasking Board not having an impact job for a late joiner.**

1. 189 of 201 raid newbies never give up (only 12 do, against 50 of 209 at prime) and only 44 lose a unit before an impact; the loss path is small at raid.
2. A joiner takes the first job in `_open`'s order: every job with a free slot, by priority, then the full ones below the overfill cap (`tasking.py:378-390`; `joinable_jobs` `tasking.py:468-481` for a bare JOIN, `_pool` `tasking.py:1106-1115` for an idle unit). Free beats overfill, whatever its kind. A strike job has `tasking.slots.strike` (2) plus at most `tasking.strike_max_scaled_slots` (3) slots (`tasking_gen.py:540`); once those are taken, a new striker takes a free PATROL (`tasking.slots.patrol` 2) or ESCORT_CONVOY (2) slot rather than overfill the strike. Neither of those is an impact in A2 unless an enemy shows up.
3. The first board job a newbie held, tail against not: STRIKE 12 against 73, PATROL 20 against 10, ESCORT_CONVOY 13 against 5, RESUPPLY 11 against 14, none (GOTO order or no job) 16 against 20 (raid seeds 1-8). Only 12 of 85 newbies whose first job was a STRIKE ended in the tail; 20 of 30 who got a PATROL and 13 of 18 who got an ESCORT did.
4. Auto newbies in the raid tail spent their first 900 s mostly in: `idle:full` 36.4 % (every job in their pool at its overfill cap, `idle_diagnosis` `tasking.py:1176`), ESCORT_CONVOY transit plus on station without a shot 21.6 %, `idle:refuel_stuck` 5.5 % (22.0 % of their whole tail time; `tasking.py:1209`, `_refuel_goal` `tasking.py:1483-1506`: a landed aircraft at a base with no stock and no hop in reach), PATROL transit 4.1 %.
5. The role kinds add a floor: at raid, capturer plus scout tail newbies are 35 of 196 judged (17.9 %), prime 24 h 28 of 207 (13.5 %): above 10 % on their own, whatever the auto newbies do.

## 5. Levers

**Lever 1 (raid, surge; a key exists): `tasking.strike_max_scaled_slots`, 3 to 6.** `x-tune` range 2 to 6 (step 1), `contracts/config/balance.json`; `balance/default.yaml` has 3. Hypothesis: more strike slots per strike job at a big crowd keep late joiners on strike (priority 40 beats PATROL 15 once a slot is free) instead of PATROL, ESCORT and `idle:full`; metric: raid and surge A2 tail and `idle` share. Probe evidence (raid-300, same 8 seeds, same sha, `--set`), with the confound stated:

| | median | tail | counted newbies | campaigns ended before 4 h |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| base, seeds 1-4 | 654 s | 41.5 % (22/53) | 56 | 4 of 4 |
| `=6`, seeds 1-4 | 254 s | 31.7 % (20/63) | 71 | 4 of 4 |
| base, seeds 1-8 | 398 s | 38.3 % (75/196) | 201 | 6 of 8 |
| `=6`, seeds 1-8 | 301 s | 28.3 % (30/106) | 123 | 8 of 8 |

The drop is about 10 percentage points and the median falls by a quarter to a half, but the tail stays far above 10 %, and the counted set changes with the run (a shorter raid has fewer late joiners; two baseline seeds hit the 4 h cap). The remaining tail is idle: 55.2 % of the tail's first 900 s (`idle:full` and `idle:refuel_stuck` about 16 % each of tail time in seeds 1-4), so this is a partial lever. It is the Q-TD-98 / NX-479 "board capacity grows with the live crowd" gap (`TRACKER.md` NX-479, open) in key form. Its wider effect on the war (strike jobs absorbing more units, raids ending sooner, `idle` of other kinds) was not measured beyond the raid A2 line; run the A/B below before any default change.

**Lever 2 (prime-50 class and up to evening-150; needs design or an owner decision, no key): the first sortie's survival and what A2 does with a newbie who left.** 71 % of the prime 24 h tail is a newbie who lost its first unit and left. Two separate handles:
- Survival of the first sortie (design): units die to Static fighters in transit a median 73 s after the JOIN; no key targets "a newbie's first route", and `crosses_ring` avoids only discovered rings, not fighters. Which Static job the killing fighters fly was not traced. Any survivability change must stay horizontal (game-designer rule: nothing that scales with rank) and needs the owner.
- The persona assumption (`profiles.yaml:63`): `rejoin_prob` 0.7 and `max_attempts` 3 are PROPOSED harness values, not balance keys, so the queue's `set:` cannot reach them. Whether A2 should count a viewer who left after dying is a definition question (Q-SIM-97 counts "none after being watched more than 900 s"); with them out, prime reads 12.7 % (24 h) and 9.8 % (6 h). I recommend an owner question, not a change here.

**Not a lever for the tail:** `join_to_spawn` (0 s), `on_task` (0 to 8 %), `transit` for auto newbies at raid (10 to 16 %).

**Other findings to route:**
- `refuel_stuck` is real at raid: 22.0 % of the raid auto tail's time (8.9 to 15.2 % of all tail time). A supply-and-stock matter (starved forward bases); needs an item, not a key.
- Capturer and scout kinds fail A2 on their own (65 to 81 % tail): their first route is 100 to 225 km and the first impact needs arrival or a new track. NX-512 and NX-513 did not bring them under 900 s in these runs. Needs a design decision (impact for a capture run, or kind shares).
