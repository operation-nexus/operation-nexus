### Findings
1. **The stalled seeds stay stalled.** s8 and s9 spend longer at 7+ in arm B (79.7 and 64.5 min against 24.5 and 0.0), but their longest stretch stays under 20 minutes, so no hold completes.
2. **Four won seeds stall under agency** (s1, s2, s3, s10). Each loses 26 to 46 strategic nodes in arm B against 1 to 5 in arm A, the same see-saw K1 describes for s8 and s9.
3. **s2 reaches a long stretch but can't finish the hold.** It holds 7+ for 122.9 minutes in one stretch, starts 2 holds, and loses 123.7 minutes of hold progress to DEFENSES_DOWN pauses. In arm B, unlike K0's arm, hold pauses do keep a seed open.
4. **More Offensives in total, but not a higher rate.** Arm B has 2.4 times the Offensives (482 against 200) and almost twice the coalition captures, but mostly because more of its seeds run the full 24 h. Per run-hour in the stalled seeds, the rates are close: 3.13 Offensives and 1.54 strategic-node losses per hour in arm B's six (450 and 222 over 144 h), against 2.94 and 1.52 in arm A's two (141 and 73 over 48 h). In the won seeds, arm B is lower on both (1.70 and 0.69 per hour against 2.01 and 0.82). (Corrected 2026-11-03 in the M0 review: the first version read the 2.4 times as a higher Offensive rate.)
5. **Tacticians mostly retake.** Of the picks that weren't a fallback, 991 of 1504 were retakes of the latest lost strategic node; answering a live enemy capture bar fired only 33 times. The 300 s window rarely catches a bar, as the spec's risks section expected.
6. **No won seed became a steamroll,** and won seeds' victory times stay between 2.27 h and 3.71 h.

## What this means

Bots that push where the fight is don't break the stall; they spread it. A seed that stalls looks the same in both arms, with about the same Offensive and loss rates per hour, but agency tips four more seeds into that state. So the open question is what tips a seed into the see-saw, not why the Static attacks faster once it is in one [UNVERIFIED].
