### Decision (lead; owner may veto)
- **Keep both switches at `fraction` for now.** Storage isn't being raised yet, and at today's storage `loadouts` M 40 costs starvation, momentum and overnight income for no gain.
- **When storage upgrades arrive** (GAMEPLAY-LATER §5a), `loadouts` with M around 40 is the version that keeps hauling flat. Re-probe then, alongside the upgrade.
