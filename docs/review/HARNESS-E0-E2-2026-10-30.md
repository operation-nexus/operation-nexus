## Findings
1. **Hold decay shortens raids on 3 of 6 seeds and leaves the other 3 at the same end tick.** Raid-300 VICTORY time, E2 (reset) → E0 (decay):
   - s1: 2.91 → 2.83 h;
   - s5: 2.70 → 2.41 h;
   - s6: 2.08 → 1.85 h;
   - s2, s3, s4: 2.48, 2.23, 2.51 h in both (same tick counts, different final state hashes).

   Median 2.5 → 2.4 h. All 6 seeds won in both, and none was flagged as a steamroll. This is the mechanism working as specified: a broken hold now keeps its progress, so retaking it finishes sooner. It does not add comebacks for the enemy.
2. **Hold breaks don't change.** Both runs have 14 victory holds with 8 broken. HOLD_AT_RISK sitreps are 15 against 16. Per-hold Static operations differ on holds 3, 11 and 14: E2 has 28, 20 and 19 against E0's 23, 5 and 8 respectively, which reads as the enemy getting more time to fight over the last holds under `reset`. How many broken holds resumed, and how many reset by reason, still can't be read: the report doesn't print `holds_resumed` or `hold_resets` (follow-up already listed).
3. **The longest raid stretch without an Offensive** is median 55.0 min (E0) against 62.8 min (E2). The differences are only in the tails of s5 and s6, which end sooner under decay.
4. **E0 reproduces D0, and E2 reproduces V2, in every raid number printed here:**
   - median h 2.4 / 2.5;
   - A2 median 801 / 640 s;
   - starved 296.3 / 313.3;
   - strike packages downed 112 / 119;
   - BRAVO dwell 2.02 / 2.12 h.

   So the D0-vs-V2 raid differences the earlier review saw come from NX-489, not from NX-490 or NX-497. NX-497 (sanctuary repair) changes no printed number at these profiles. The word SANCTUARY doesn't appear in either report.
5. **Raid A2 is worse under decay:** median 801 s against 640 s, with the share over 900 s at 48.3% (69/143) against 48.0% (71/148). E0 counts 147 newbies against E2's 152, which fits shorter raids. The cause is [UNVERIFIED] (pooled seeds, H3).
6. **Prime-50 is identical in every line printed,** apart from wall time and final state hashes. No victory hold forms at prime-50 (0 holds), so `hold_break` has nothing to act on there. HQ captures are 0 in all four profile runs.
