### Findings
1. **NX-471 ends seed 1's stall** (corrected 11-02: J0 still stalls 2 of 12). s1, the seed that stalled in G0, G2 and now I1, wins at 3.76 h with the seizure on. All 6 seeds win, and none is flagged as a steamroll. Why it closes is [UNVERIFIED]. The likely path: a node the Static retakes now comes back to it Disabled, so it can't defend the retake while it repairs, and the hold-pause loop that kept s1 open never forms.
2. **Seeds 2–6 are about the same with the rule on or off.**
   - Offensives: 25 with it on, 23 with it off.
   - A2 medians and idle shares overlap seed by seed.
   - Victory times are mixed: s2 is shorter with the rule on, s4 longer.
3. **The pooled drop in contest is an artifact of the stalled seed.** I1's s1 ran 24 h and carries 74 of its 97 Offensives and 286 of its 445 A2 newbies. Removing that one long fight is what moves the pooled holds, Offensives, A2 (636 s against 239 s) and idle (32% against 17%). The first read of I0 against G0 and G2 took this for a loss of contest. It isn't one.
4. **The Static loses more income to the storage clip with NX-471 on:** 22069 of 54095 due, against 46221 of 138625. Per run-hour that is about equal, because the runs are shorter. No action.
