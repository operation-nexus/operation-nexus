## 8. Capture

  - (from **An HQ is very hard to capture, hardest alone (in code, NX-490; Q-TD-105 owner 2026-10-29, Q-TD-127 lead, owner may veto).**) Derived from the shipped values (world s, ticks at `sim.tick_hz` 10 and `sim.time_scale` 6, 0.6 world s a tick; PROPOSED, not measured): at an HQ with no defender and quiet 1.0, 1 capturer takes 1200 s (2000 ticks, 3.3 game min) against 60 s (100 ticks) today; 4 take 300 s (500 ticks); 10 take 120 s (200 ticks); 20 take 60 s (100 ticks), the time 1 capturer needs at an empty base. With an unarmed defender there (× `garrison_mult` 3) a lone capturer takes 7200 s (12,001 ticks, 20 game min). A lone bar gains 50,000 millionths a tick and drains 500,000 when contested or abandoned (`decay_per_s`), so one tick of contest undoes ten of lone progress.
    - (under **Repair under the new owner.**) **Why this mechanism (Q-TD-135).** It reuses the stock-paid repair, so supply still decides, as the owner asked for Q-TD-86. It keeps "faster at bigger bases" (Q-GD-27), because the per-kind rates are divided, not replaced. A timer would count down with no supply. Slowing all facility repair would also lengthen the SEAD suppression window that Q-TD-86 decided (39 world s at an airbase).
  - (under **Facilities at a captured base (Q-GP-25, amended by Q-TD-78 / NX-471; Q-TD-135 lead, owner may veto; in code, NX-471):**) **How long (PROPOSED; derived from the shipped keys, not measured).** At `sim.tick_hz` 10 and `sim.time_scale` 6, a tick is 0.6 world s and 0.1 game s. The base rate is 600 thousandths a tick at an AIRBASE, HQ or PORT (`logistics.repair_rate` 1.0) and 300 at a FOB (0.5).
    - **Off-line window.** From the floor to the restore line is 39 hp, or 39,000 thousandths. At S 92:
      - AIRBASE, HQ or PORT: 600 ÷ 92 a tick, so 5,980 ticks (65 × S): **10.0 game minutes**.
      - FOB: 300 ÷ 92 a tick, so 11,960 ticks: **19.9 game minutes**.
    - **Then to full hp.** The remaining 97 hp take 162 more ticks (16.2 game s) at an airbase and 324 (32.4 game s) at a FOB.
    - **Today.** With the switch off, or for a struck site (Q-TD-86), the floor to the restore line takes 65 ticks at an airbase: 39 world s, 6.5 game s.
    - **Sweep range** (window at an airbase / at a FOB): S 92 gives 10.0 / 19.9 min, S 100 gives 10.8 / 21.7, S 200 gives 21.7 / 43.3, and S 300 gives 32.5 / 65.
    - **Stock.** The price is unchanged: 3.9 stock to the restore line and 13.6 to full, drawn in whole units as the carries reach 1 (`rearm.py:26-38`).
