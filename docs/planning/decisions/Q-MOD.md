## Combat and simulation feel (added 2026-10-17 at owner's request)

| ID | Question | Recommended default | Blocks | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q-MOD-5 | Automated anti-griefing (owner idea): disruptive patterns lead to escalating consequences, including XP loss and eventual timeouts or bans | **Conduct score, separate from skill.** Only *clear disruptive patterns* are flagged, never losing or being new: suicide spam (repeated deaths in a short window with no objective progress), command spam / throttle abuse, rapid role-flipping, repeated blocked-callsign attempts, draining a starved base on purpose. Flags lower a decaying Conduct score. **Escalation ladder** (slow, reversible): (1) a gentle warning in the digest → (2) reduced capabilities (longer cooldowns, no rally calls) → (3) temporary in-game timeout → (4) XP/merit penalty → (5) flagged to mods for a ban decision. **Bans always need a human.** Conduct recovers with good play over time. Every step is logged, visible to mods, and reversible on appeal. Thresholds PROPOSED, admin-tunable. | ✅ 2026-10-17: A, Conduct system as proposed. **Transparency:** the rules are published (site + !rules); each player sees their own conduct standing and any active restriction (via !me and later the public site). Others' scores are not shown. Mods and admins see everything in the Command Center (conduct, merit, stats, rankings). Indicator to other players: Q-MOD-6 |
| Q-MOD-6 | How should other players be made aware of a low-conduct player? | Show **no score**. While a restriction is active, that player's unit carries a small neutral **"probation" tag** (no shaming text, no numbers). It disappears when the restriction ends. That avoids pile-ons while still informing the team. | ✅ 2026-10-17: A, neutral probation tag only while a restriction is active; no scores shown |
