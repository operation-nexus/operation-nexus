| Q-GD-18 | Capture rules | Ground and logistics units capture uncontested zones; time configurable, scaled by units present (diminishing returns); aircraft can't capture | P1 | ✅ 2026-10-17: A, plus **base defenses (HP)**: a base's defense HP must be knocked to 0 (bombing, artillery, tank fire) before capture can start.
