## Combat and simulation feel (added 2026-10-17 at owner's request)

| ID | Question | Recommended default | Blocks | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q-CBT-1 | Damage model: just alive/dead, or damage states? | Damage states: **Healthy → Damaged** (slower, reduced sensors) → **Mission-kill** (must RTB, can be repaired) → **Destroyed**. More drama, and it makes repair and logistics matter. | P1 | ✅ 2026-10-17: A, damage states: Healthy → Damaged → Mission-kill (RTB, repairable) → Destroyed. Disabled added between Mission-kill and Destroyed (Q-GP-8); first-build wrecks: Q-CBT-14. |
| Q-CBT-2 | Which factors change hit chance (Pk)? | Track quality (blob / track / radar lock / painted), range vs weapon envelope, target evasion and altitude band, jamming on either side, countermeasures (chaff/flares, one-shot), terrain masking, and weather/night (later). Each is a multiplier in config. | P1 | ✅ 2026-10-17: A, full factor set as multipliers in config; per-platform attributes feed it. First build and cap: Q-CBT-10; radar lock dropped until its unit arrives. |
| Q-CBT-9 | Penalties for losing a unit? | **No XP loss or personal shaming** (newcomer-friendly, anti-toxic). Consequences are team and logistics: the lost unit's cost comes out of base stock, a modest respawn delay that grows with repeated losses in a short window (anti-suicide-spam), and repair time at base for damaged units. Rescued crews remove part of the delay. | ✅ 2026-10-17: A for normal losses (team and logistics consequences only). Disruptive behavior is handled separately by the Conduct system (Q-MOD-5) |
| Q-CBT-3 | Show hit chances to viewers? | Show them in the PiP intercept scope ("Pk 62%") during engagements. It teaches the system and builds trust that outcomes are fair. | ✅ 2026-10-17: A, show Pk in the PiP intercept scope during engagements |
| Q-CBT-4 | Friendly fire? | No in v1 (co-op, avoids griefing) | P1 | ✅ 2026-10-17: Off in v1. **Becomes a per-campaign option** (set at campaign setup, never toggled live mid-war), default off, editable by the owner or an Admin only, never a GM (Q-ALN-10). Recommended use: special event campaigns. |
| Q-CBT-5 | Weather and day/night cycle? | Later (P4): a visual day/night cycle plus weather fronts that reduce sensor range and Pk. Seeded and visible ahead of time. | ✅ 2026-10-17: A, later (P4): day/night + weather fronts affecting sensors and Pk, forecast on screen. **Fully configurable** per campaign/theater in admin (on/off, intensity, cycle length) |
| Q-CBT-6 | Retreat behavior when badly damaged? | Units auto-RTB at mission-kill. Player-controlled units can override with `!target` but risk destruction. | ✅ 2026-10-17: A by default (auto-RTB at mission-kill if still mobile), plus an **operator stance/intent** set at deploy or later: `press` (fight to the end, no auto-RTB), `standard` (default), `cautious` (RTB earlier). E.g. `!join strike press` or `!stance press` |
| Q-CBT-7 | Ammo per sortie? | Yes. Limited munitions per sortie (from base stock), so rearming and logistics matter. Guns are unlimited but weak. | ✅ 2026-10-17: A, limited munitions per sortie drawn from base stock. **Guns are limited too** (high round count). Rounds used are charged to base stock on resupply. **Out of munitions → auto-RTB** unless stance `press` or the operator orders guns-only (`!stance guns`). **Unlimited ammo is a campaign/test option** (testing, special events), owner or an Admin only, never a GM (Q-ALN-10) |
| Q-CBT-8 | Critical or lucky hits? | Small seeded chance of an outsized effect (e.g. a radar knocked offline for a while), shown with a callout. Adds stories without deciding wars. | ✅ 2026-10-17: A, small seeded chance of outsized effects with a callout; configurable |

Cap 0.95 so every shot can miss. `radar_lock` dropped from the first build; it and every unbuilt factor are a registered 1.0 fallback (FB-6) that returns with its unit (owner: don't let it drop). Masking applies to NOE, LOW and ground targets (Q-FOW-3). COMBAT §5.3. | P1 | ✅ 2026-10-20 |
